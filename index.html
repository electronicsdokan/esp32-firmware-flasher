FIELD GUIDE

Help & troubleshooting

Connect an ESP32

Use a USB data cable, close other serial tools, then choose Connect device. On many boards, hold BOOT, tap RESET/EN, release RESET, then release BOOT. The exact sequence varies by board.

Flash firmware

Select a local .bin and confirm its flash address. Connect the board, then choose Flash firmware. Progress is reported by esptool-js from the actual write callback.

Serial monitor

Choose the baud rate used by your firmware, usually 115200. Send commands from the prompt with Enter. Chrome or Edge is required for Web Serial.

If connection fails

Try bootloader mode, another USB cable, or close Arduino/PlatformIO/PuTTY. A port can only be owned by one application at a time.
